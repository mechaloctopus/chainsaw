STIHL model codes look like a system, and mostly they are one. But the letter
suffixes have been reused, retired and redefined across decades, so a code on
a vintage saw may not mean what it means today. This page says so rather than
pretending otherwise.

<Block kicker="A word about the T suffix">

<StopWork trigger="a top-handle saw and no rope training" who="a certified arborist">
`T` means top handle: a saw built to be run one-handed by a roped arborist
who is hanging in a tree. It is light, it is fast, and it has the highest
injury rate per hour of any saw sold.

If you are standing on the ground, a top-handle saw is the wrong tool, and
"just for the small stuff" is exactly how people lose fingers with one.
